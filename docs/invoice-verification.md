# Invoice verification

- `/verify`: public verification-code lookup, linked from the main navigation and footer.
- `/verify/[code]`: public invoice summary; the 32-character random code is generated on issue.
- `/verify/[code]/pdf`: downloads an A4 invoice PDF with itemized charges, receipts, outstanding balance, legal note, and an embedded verification QR.
- `/verify/manage`: password-protected staff console for creating/editing invoices, recording received payments, marking the remaining balance paid, and downloading PDFs or standalone QR codes.

Configure the project-wide `ADMIN_PASSWORD` and `SESSION_SECRET` once (see [admin configuration](admin-auth.md)). The signing secret must contain at least 32 characters; the invoice console uses it for its signed, HTTP-only session lasting 12 hours. It uses the existing `FIREBASE_ADMIN_PROJECT_ID`, `FIREBASE_ADMIN_CLIENT_EMAIL`, and `FIREBASE_ADMIN_PRIVATE_KEY` credentials.

Records live in Firestore's `verifiedInvoices` collection and are accessed only through the server's Admin SDK. A yearly counter at `verificationCounters/invoices-YYYY` allocates numbers in the format `ACT-INV/YYYY/000001`. The counter, invoice creation, and collision check run in one Firestore transaction. Existing manual numbers are preserved and skipped if they collide with a generated number. Numbers cannot be edited or supplied by the client. Existing Firestore rules deny client access to both collections; no rules deployment or composite index is required.

Issue an invoice in the console and download its PDF. Production PDFs encode the canonical `https://www.activid.id/verify/[code]` address; development PDFs use the local origin. The optional standalone QR PNG uses the origin where the console is open. Updating an invoice preserves its code and number, so previously printed QR codes continue to work. PDFs are generated from the current stored record with a snapshot timestamp and are never cached. Public records use fresh reads, and saving invalidates the route. Invoice details, PDF downloads, and admin routes are excluded from search indexing.

Invoices use whole IDR unit prices, positive whole quantities, and a date sent per line item. Amount inputs show an `Rp` prefix and Indonesian thousands separators, while submitting raw integer rupiah values. Received payments have a description (for example, down payment or balance payment), an optional known date, and a positive IDR amount. The server rejects overpayments and derives unpaid, partially paid, or paid status from receipts. The quick "Mark paid" action transactionally records only the outstanding balance; repeating it cannot duplicate that payment. Older paid invoices are represented by a legacy receipt with an unknown date, preserving their existing balance.

The Indonesian legal note cites Article 5 of the ITE law, as amended by [Law No. 1 of 2024](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/884/t/undangundang%20nomor%201%20tahun%202024). Recognition of electronic evidence is conditional on statutory requirements. The QR verifies the current ACTIVID record; it is not a certified electronic signature or an unconditional assurance of legal validity. Certified signatures have separate requirements under [Government Regulation No. 71 of 2019, Articles 59-60](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/695/t/peraturan%20pemerintah%20nomor%2071%20tahun%202019). These records are public to anyone holding the link; include only recipient-facing details.

Invoice edits carry a revision number and update in a transaction. Stale edits are rejected so they cannot silently overwrite payments recorded by another tab or the quick action.
